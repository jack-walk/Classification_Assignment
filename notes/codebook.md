# Codebook

We're labeling *videos from former West Virginia Gov. Jim Justice's official YouTube channel* for appearances of his pet bulldog, BabyDog.

Label each video as a whole: one label per video. You can speed through the video to look for images of a bulldog, often found sitting in a wheelbarrow or chair.

Here are some examples:
Sample of BabyDog in a chair: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h7TIqQDYZQs @ 04:11
Sample of BabyDog in a chair, from afar: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3F-zvCYe6-8 @ 0:01
Sample of BabyDog in a wheelbarrow: https://www.instagram.com/p/DCUe_-uAWiA/?img_index=2

Any time a bulldog appears in a video, we can assume it is BabyDog. However, we only want to count instances where BabyDog is physically present in the video.

This does not include:
- Photographs of a dog
- Illustrations of a dog
- B
-> Label these, and any instances where Babydog is not physically present in the video, as False for "features_politician"

If you are uncertain, press "s" to skip and add a note explaining why, so you can revisit it with other annotators.
